# Planes through the centre of a polytope, in Lean

Lean 4 (Mathlib v4.35.0-rc2) proofs of Theorems 1 (one direction), 2, 3 (i) and 4 of DICTO's paper #120,
*The Vacuus and the Resonus: Form and Emptiness in the Planes of Polyhedra* (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24521888),
and the lattice-web test of its next version. Statements: `Challenge.lean`; proofs: `Solution.lean` from the library
`VacuusResonus/`. Description and sources: `formalization.yaml`. With Claude (Anthropic).
